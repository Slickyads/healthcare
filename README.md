## Clients

### Palmcare Medical Clinic (Dubai)
- **Type:** Multi-specialty medical clinic in Amaya Mall, Liwan, Dubai
- **Services:** Dental, laser therapy, aesthetics, cosmetics, lab testing, general medicine
- **Channels managed:** Meta (Facebook/Instagram) + Google Ads (Search, PMax, Brand)
- **Key metrics (Aug 2026):**
  - Google spend: 6,568 AED → 2,787 conversions at 3.28 AED avg CPC
  - Meta spend: 4,968 AED → 93 messaging conversations, 108 leads
  - Top Google keywords: `medical center`, `عيادة بالقرب مني`, `palm care polyclinic`
  - Core Meta audience: females aged 25–44 (Instagram primary)
  - Top locations: Liwan (5-mile radius), Nad Al Sheba, Dubai Motor City
